# Data dictionary

All data in `public/sample-data/` is **synthetic**, generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py` (seed 42, reproducible). The business, "Zarnab Tiles (Pvt) Ltd", and every customer name are invented. Currency: Pakistani rupees (PKR). Dates: ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.

| File | Rows | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| `customers.csv` | 120 | 20 distributors, 70 dealers, 18 key accounts, 12 institutional projects across 4 regions and 15 cities |
| `orders.csv` | 8,189 | 1 Jan 2024 – 30 Sep 2026; 20 products in 5 categories; 119 cancelled |
| `invoices.csv` | 8,070 | One per delivered order |
| `targets.csv` | 132 | 33 months × 4 regions |
| `complaints.csv` | 240 | 7 categories, 4 owning teams |

The data snapshot date is **30 Sep 2026**: payments after it are unknown, so recent invoices are open.

## How the synthetic data was generated

- **Volume:** Poisson order counts per customer and month, driven by customer type, region, a construction-season pattern (weaker in the monsoon months and Ramadan) and ~6% annual volume growth. List prices rise 6% per year.
- **Scale:** quarterly net sales of roughly PKR 450–930 million, in the range of a mid-sized Pakistani tile business.
- **Payments:** each customer has a typical delay after the due date; a small share of invoices are only partly paid (disputes).
- **Targets:** derived from the planned run-rate (without the Q3-2026 push), so target attainment is meaningful.

### Deliberate storylines (so the analytics have something real to find)

1. **Q3-2026 sales push:** extra orders into South and Central distributors near quarter-end, with higher discounts. Sales beat target.
2. **Slower South payments:** South-region distributors pay ~45 days later on invoices from June 2026, so collections fall behind target and overdue balances grow.
3. **Margin squeeze:** estimated costs rise ~8% from Jan 2026; "Porcelain 60x60 Polished Beige" carries ~7 points of extra discount from Apr 2026.
4. **Complaint spike:** Aug–Sep 2026 shade-variation complaints on "GVT 60x120 Marble Glossy".
5. **Chronic slow payers:** two accounts (C0104, C0107) with long delays and disputed balances; set to "On Hold".
6. **Cancelled orders:** ~1.5% of orders, excluded from sales.

The tests confirm that the engine finds storylines 1, 3 and 4 (`tests/intelligence.test.ts`).

## Known data-quality limitations

- One payment record per invoice; no credit notes, returns or unapplied cash.
- One product per order; multi-line orders would need an order-lines table.
- Costs are estimates.
- Customer region and channel are fixed over time.
- Complaint resolution dates depend on status upkeep.
