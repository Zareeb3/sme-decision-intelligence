# Data dictionary

This page describes the **canonical datasets** the analytics engine reads. Company data reaches this shape in one of three ways: CSV upload with automatic column matching, the SAP pipeline (`pipeline/sap_pipeline.py`, see [SAP_INTEGRATION.md](SAP_INTEGRATION.md)), or any tool that posts to the ingest API. The sections first describe the import rules for real company data, then the bundled sample.

## Importing company data

| Dataset | File | Required? | Required fields | Key |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sales lines | `orders.csv` | Yes | order_id, order_date, customer_id, product_id, quantity_sqm, net_sales_pkr | order_id |
| Customers | `customers.csv` | Yes | customer_id, customer_name, region | customer_id |
| Invoices | `invoices.csv` | Yes | invoice_id, customer_id, invoice_date, invoice_amount_pkr | invoice_id |
| Payments received | `payments.csv` | Optional (recommended) | invoice_id, payment_date, amount_pkr | payment_id (generated if absent) |
| Monthly targets | `targets.csv` | Optional | month, region, sales_target_pkr | month + region |
| Complaints | `complaints.csv` | Optional | complaint_id, date_opened, customer_id, category | complaint_id |

Field names ending in `_pkr` and `_sqm` are historical: amounts are in the company currency chosen in Settings and quantities in its volume unit.

**What the importer handles automatically**

- **Column names.** Business names ("Billing Document", "Sold-to party", "Net value") and SAP technical names (VBELN, FKDAT, KUNAG, MATNR, FKIMG, NETWR, WAVWR, FKART, KUNNR, NAME1, BELNR, AUGDT, REBZG, QMNUM and more) are matched to fields. Users can change any match; the choice is remembered per file layout.
- **File formats.** Comma, semicolon, tab and pipe delimiters; SAP list exports with ruler lines; UTF-8 BOM.
- **Numbers.** Thousands separators and SAP trailing minus (`1,250.00-`).
- **Dates.** `YYYY-MM-DD`, `DD.MM.YYYY`, `DD/MM/YYYY`, `MM/DD/YYYY` and `YYYYMMDD`. The format is detected per column from the values; ambiguous columns are read as day-first and reported.
- **Codes.** SAP billing types (F2 = invoice, G2/RE = credit note, S1/S2 = cancellation) and common status words.
- **Missing optional fields** get documented defaults: order region and channel from the customer; due date from invoice date + payment terms; product name from product ID; category "Uncategorised"; list price derived from net sales and discount.
- **Credit notes.** A sales line with status "Credit Note" (or negative net value) reduces net sales and quantity. In payments, `payment_type = Credit Note` reduces the invoice balance but is **not** counted as cash collected.

Every import shows how many rows were valid, the notes on any defaults applied, and row-level errors. Invalid files never replace current data; the previous version of each dataset is kept for one-click restore.

## payments.csv — key: `payment_id`

| Column | Type | Rules | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| `payment_id` | text | unique; generated if missing | e.g. FI clearing document |
| `invoice_id` | text | required | The invoice paid (SAP: REBZG or the cleared item) |
| `payment_date` | date | valid date | Value / posting date of the receipt |
| `amount_pkr` | number | > 0 | Amount applied to the invoice |
| `payment_type` | enum | Payment, Credit Note | Credit notes reduce the balance but are not collections |

When a payments file is loaded, it replaces the single `amount_paid_pkr`/`payment_date` on invoices, so several part-payments per invoice are handled. Without it, the invoice columns are used (one payment per invoice).

## The bundled sample

All data in `public/sample-data/` is **synthetic**, generated by `scripts/generate_synthetic_data.py` (seed 42, reproducible). The business, "Zarnab Tiles (Pvt) Ltd", and every customer name are invented. Currency: Pakistani rupees (PKR). Dates: ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`.

| File | Rows | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| `customers.csv` | 120 | 20 distributors, 70 dealers, 18 key accounts, 12 institutional projects across 4 regions and 15 cities |
| `orders.csv` | 8,189 | 1 Jan 2024 – 30 Sep 2026; 20 products in 5 categories; 119 cancelled |
| `invoices.csv` | 8,070 | One per delivered order |
| `targets.csv` | 132 | 33 months × 4 regions |
| `complaints.csv` | 240 | 7 categories, 4 owning teams |

The data snapshot date is **30 Sep 2026**: payments after it are unknown, so recent invoices are open.

## How the synthetic data was generated

- **Volume:** Poisson order counts per customer and month, driven by customer type, region, a construction-season pattern (weaker in the monsoon months and Ramadan) and ~6% annual volume growth. List prices rise 6% per year.
- **Scale:** quarterly net sales of roughly PKR 450–930 million, in the range of a mid-sized Pakistani tile business.
- **Payments:** each customer has a typical delay after the due date; a small share of invoices are only partly paid (disputes).
- **Targets:** derived from the planned run-rate (without the Q3-2026 push), so target attainment is meaningful.

### Deliberate storylines (so the analytics have something real to find)

1. **Q3-2026 sales push:** extra orders into South and Central distributors near quarter-end, with higher discounts. Sales beat target.
2. **Slower South payments:** South-region distributors pay ~45 days later on invoices from June 2026, so collections fall behind target and overdue balances grow.
3. **Margin squeeze:** estimated costs rise ~8% from Jan 2026; "Porcelain 60x60 Polished Beige" carries ~7 points of extra discount from Apr 2026.
4. **Complaint spike:** Aug–Sep 2026 shade-variation complaints on "GVT 60x120 Marble Glossy".
5. **Chronic slow payers:** two accounts (C0104, C0107) with long delays and disputed balances; set to "On Hold".
6. **Cancelled orders:** ~1.5% of orders, excluded from sales.

The tests confirm that the engine finds storylines 1, 3 and 4 (`tests/intelligence.test.ts`).

## Known data-quality limitations

- The sample has one payment record per invoice and no credit notes. Company data can include both through `payments.csv` and credit-note sales lines; unapplied cash (payments not matched to an invoice) is not modelled.
- One product per order; multi-line orders would need an order-lines table.
- Costs are estimates.
- Customer region and channel are fixed over time.
- Complaint resolution dates depend on status upkeep.
